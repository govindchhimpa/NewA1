Iceland is slowly being torn in half.

The island sits right on top of the mid-Atlantic ridge, where the North American plate and the Eurasian plate pull apart.

They move about 2 cm a year, roughly as fast as your fingernails grow.

At a place called Thingvellir, you can actually walk through the gap between the two continents.

All that pressure makes Iceland one of the most volcanic places on Earth.

It has around 30 active volcanic systems for only about 380,000 people.

But there is a bonus.

The heat underground warms about 9 out of 10 Icelandic homes.
