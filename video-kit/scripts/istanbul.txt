One city sits on two continents.

Istanbul is split in half by a waterway called the Bosphorus, about 30 kilometers long.

The western side is in Europe, and the eastern side is in Asia.

More than 15 million people live there.

Every day, many of them travel from one continent to the other, just to get to work.

They use bridges, tunnels, and ferries.

The city was once the capital of three great empires, the Roman, the Byzantine, and the Ottoman, because whoever controlled this strait controlled the trade between two worlds.
