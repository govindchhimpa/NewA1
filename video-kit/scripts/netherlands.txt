About a quarter of the Netherlands is below sea level.

The lowest point sits around 7 meters under the sea.

Without protection, much of the country would be underwater.

So, the Dutch built dykes, dams, and giant pumps.

After a terrible flood in 1953 killed more than 1800 people, they built one of the biggest flood defenses in the world, called the Delta Works.

They even created new land.

A large part of the province of Flevoland was open sea until the 1950s and 60s.

The name of the country literally means the Low Countries.
