One narrow strip of water carries about one-fifth of the world's oil.

It is the Strait of Hormuz between Iran and Oman.

At its narrowest, it is only about 39 kilometers wide.

It connects the Persian Gulf to the open ocean.

Almost every oil tanker leaving that region must pass through it.

The shipping lanes are even smaller.

Giant tankers sail in two lanes only a few kilometers wide, one going in and one coming out.

So a tiny piece of water on the map matters for the whole planet.
