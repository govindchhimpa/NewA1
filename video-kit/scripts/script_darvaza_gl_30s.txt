In nineteen seventy-one, so the story goes, Soviet engineers were drilling for oil in Turkmenistan when the ground collapsed.
They had broken into a cavern of natural gas, and a crater opened, about seventy meters wide.
To stop the poisonous gas spreading, they set it on fire.
The plan was simple. Burn it for a few weeks, and the gas would run out.
They were wrong.
More than fifty years later, the Gates of Hell is still burning, though the fire is finally fading.
