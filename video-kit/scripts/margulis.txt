1967.

Biologist Lynn Margulis proposes something that sounds absurd.

Long ago, the parts that power our cells were once separate bacteria.

Her idea is that a large cell swallowed a small one, and instead of digesting it, the two began to live together.

Her paper is rejected by about 15 journals.

Finally, one journal agrees to print it.

Even then, most scientists ignore it for years.

But later, researchers look at the DNA inside those cell parts.

It matches bacteria.

In 1999, Margulis receives the National Medal of Science.

Every cell in your body is proof that she was right.
