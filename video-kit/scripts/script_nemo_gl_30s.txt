The nearest humans to this spot are not on Earth.

This is Point Nemo, in the South Pacific, almost twenty-seven hundred kilometers from any land. When the Space Station passes overhead, its astronauts are closer than anyone on the ground.

That is why it is a graveyard. Space agencies steer dying spacecraft here, far from every city, and let the ocean take them. Hundreds already rest on the seafloor.

The loneliest place on Earth is where our rockets go to die.
