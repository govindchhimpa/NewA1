Russia has 11 time zones. It is the biggest country on Earth, around 17 million square kilometers. That is even bigger than the surface of Pluto. When people in the far west are eating breakfast, people on the Kamchatka coast in the east are already 10 hours into the day and are about to go to bed. The Trans-Siberian Railway crosses almost all of it. The whole trip is more than 9,000 kilometers and takes about a week. Most Russians live in the west, so the huge east is mostly empty land.
