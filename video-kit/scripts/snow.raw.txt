1854. Collara is killing hundreds of people in one London neighborhood. Doctors say it spreads through bad air. Dr. John Snow does not believe it. He thinks the sickness comes from the water. So he walks the streets and he marks every death on a map. The dots form a cluster and in the middle of it sits one water pump on Broad Street. Snow brings his map to the local officials and begs them to act. They remove the pump handle and the outbreak slows. But the medical world still refuses to believe him. A committee rejects his theory. Snow dies in 1858, never knowing he was right. Years later, scientists prove that cholera is caused by germs in water. Today, his map is taught in every public health class.
