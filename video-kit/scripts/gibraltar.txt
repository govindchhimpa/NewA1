Europe and Africa are only 14 kilometers apart.

That gap is the Strait of Gibraltar between Spain and Morocco.

On a clear day, you can stand in Spain and see another continent.

It is also the only natural door between the Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea.

Around 100,000 ships squeeze through it every year.

The ancient Greeks called its two rocky sides the Pillars of Hercules and believed they marked the edge of the known world.

On the northern side stands the Rock of Gibraltar.

It has belonged to Britain since 1713, even though it sits on the Spanish coast.

Today, a strong swimmer can cross the strait in a few hours.
