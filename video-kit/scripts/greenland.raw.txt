On most maps, Greenland looks as big as Africa. It is not even close. Africa is about 30 million square kilometers. Greenland is only about 2 million. Africa is roughly 14 times bigger. The reason is the map itself. In 1569, a map maker designed a flat map for sailors, and it stretches everything near the poles. The closer a country is to the top or bottom, the bigger it looks. Russia, Canada, and Europe look huge. Africa, near the middle, looks smaller than it really is. So the map you grew up with has been quietly lying to you. Which map did you grow up with?
