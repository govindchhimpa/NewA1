The smallest country in the world fits inside a city park.

Vatican City covers less than half a square kilometer.

That is about eight times smaller than Central Park in New York.

It sits inside the city of Rome and fewer than 1,000 people live there.

It became an independent country in 1929.

Yet, it has its own post office, its own bank, its own newspaper, and its own army. the Swiss Guard.

You can walk across the entire country in about 10 minutes.
